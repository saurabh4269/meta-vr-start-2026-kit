# Palmverse - trailer script

Standalone copy of section 7 of [trailer-scripts.md](trailer-scripts.md). Same format and the same shared recipe and rules (see that file's top section).

Palmverse is the cosmic working title of the palmistry idea formerly called Hast Rekha ([idea spec](../research/ideas-7-8.md), [design pack](../research/hast-rekha-magic-design.md), [project index](../research/hast-rekha/README.md)).
The title carries no Indian branding; Indian and Vedic theming lives inside the worlds. Say "the tradition says" and treat it as play and reflection: no predictions, no health, money or relationship claims, no advice.
Stills quoted as "Ref:" are in [`research/hast-rekha/reference/magic-wonders/`](../research/hast-rekha/reference/magic-wonders/README.md). They are a **style and pacing mood board only** (their footage is a phone app and a live-action promo with faces): borrow the light, glow and timing, never the images, and keep **no faces** in ours (first-person only).

**Target length:** 60 s (a 30 s cut follows)

**Hook:** "Your palm is a map. Hold it up."

### Music and sound direction

- **Bed:** a quiet tanpura drone (Sa-Pa-Sa-low Sa), about -22 dB under the voice, with no beat and no melody. A free CC0 option is on [Freesound](https://freesound.org/people/MieliTietty/sounds/619263/) or the synthetic drone in [`research/hast-rekha-audio/`](../research/hast-rekha-audio/README.md). Keep the licence file.
- **Line ignition:** one temple-bell note per line, rising (Sa, Re, Ga, Pa, Dha). Hover: a faint shimmer rising in pitch. Touch: a bell strike at the contact point plus a soft sub thump. Select: a singing-bowl strike and a low drop. CC0 bells and bowls: [bells](https://freesound.org/people/LoopUdu/sounds/271627/), [bowl](https://freesound.org/people/hollandm/sounds/573805/).
- **Portal bloom:** 0.5 s reverse swell into the bowl strike; the room goes quiet except the narrator and drone.
- **Divine narrator:** use the chain in [`hast-rekha-audio/divine.sh`](../research/hast-rekha-audio/divine.sh) (octave-down sub, octave-up whisper, fifth pad, stone-hall reverb). The narrator voice must be a designed or stock synthetic voice, never a clone of a real person. If you use ElevenLabs, use a paid plan so no ElevenLabs text or credit goes in the video title; never show its name on screen. If you use the offline Piper voice, credit it as text in the description (see the audio README).
- **Duck:** drone and bells -6 dB under any speech. Final loudness about -14 LUFS for YouTube.
- **Silence is a shot:** leave 0.5 s of near-silence before the end-card name.

**Prep and capture**
- **Mode:** mixed reality for beats 1-4, 6-7 and the end still; the worlds in beats 5 and 8 are virtual with a passthrough hand window (see the design pack, section 2.4).
- **Room and props:** a plain wall, a matte table or lap, one soft warm lamp. Cover or remove anything branded; wear a plain long sleeve (no logos) or bare forearm. No faces, no other people. A Magic-Wonders-style warm room tone is fine; their faces and props are not.
- **Capture:** headset Camera app, 1920×1080 landscape, stabilization Low (so the glow does not smear), or MQDH Cast Cinematic 16:9 at 60 fps on Windows. Pull files with `adb pull`. Do not let any system menu, controller, Meta logo or the permission dialog show on the recording.
- **Honesty:** the five lines are placed at their classic positions from the hand's joints, not detected from the user's real creases. Say "five classic lines" (as in the VO), not "reads your lines". If Scan mode is not working, do not imply it.
- **Description text (add as plain text):** "Palmverse is a cultural entertainment inspired by palm-reading traditions. It does not predict anything and gives no advice." Credit the Freesound items, the Poly Haven HDRIs, any CC BY model authors and, if used, the offline voice (Alba, CC BY 4.0), all as text.
- **Name check:** ⚠ "Palmverse" is also used by an unrelated NFT community (a social handle and the palmverse.co domain). That is not a VR app, but check the Horizon Store, trademark and domain situation before it is the final public name; it can stay a working title for the video.
- **Fallbacks:** if Trace mode (beat 7) is not built, replace it with the same palm-up flow on the **other** hand ending in the Head-line starfield (reuse shot 9) and keep the VO "Open another line, and watch it become a sky." If the passthrough hand window (beat 6) is not built, drop the first sentence of that VO ("Your own hand stays real.") and show the fist closing in plain MR.

**Must build first to film this** (cut from the bottom; see the design pack, section 7.3)
1. Palm-up detection and the ignition of five lines in sequence with chimes (the hook and beat 2).
2. Hover, touch and pinch on a line, with the ripple, bell and chip (beats 3 and 4).
3. The portal sphere that blooms from the palm into the Heart world (lotus ocean), with the divine narrator line and subtitle.
4. Fist close that collapses the world and relights the lines.
5. The Head world (constellation sky), cheap and procedural; also used as the trace payoff.
6. Passthrough tint and the hand-in-world window (beat 6).
7. Trace mode: fingertip path above the palm becomes a glowing line (beat 7) ⚠.
8. Quick Life, Fate and Sun worlds for the flash cuts (banyan and river, Saturn path, solar mandala). Each can be a 4 s hero view only.
