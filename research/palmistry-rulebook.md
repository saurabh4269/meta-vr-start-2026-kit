# Hast Rekha rulebook

Readable version of [`data/palmistry-rules.json`](../data/palmistry-rules.json) (generated by `scripts/palm/build_rulebook.py`; edit `scripts/palm/rules_data.py`, not this file).

**63 features:** 18 lines, 2 line-attributes, 11 mounts, 1 mount-attributes, 8 hand-shapes, 7 fingers, 16 marks.

> Palmistry is a cultural / entertainment tradition, widely viewed as pseudoscience with many conflicting readings. The app says "the tradition says…" and never predicts, diagnoses or advises. Rules about death, illness, insanity, infidelity or fertility are recorded but flagged and **must not be shown**.

## How to read the citations

- `read`: I read the passage and paraphrased it.
- `toc`: section confirmed from the book's table of contents; the rule is standard teaching consistent with it, **not line-verified**.
- `web`: modern web page, facts only.
- S1 pages are printed page numbers of the section start; S2 (Benham) is cited by Part/chapter.

**Important Indian-tradition note.** The classical Indian texts (S3, S4, S5) read the palm through a few described lines and above all *auspicious marks* (fish, conch, wheel, lotus, flag, swastika…). The heart/head lines and the planetary mounts (parvat) of modern hast-rekha come from the Western Cheiro/Benham tradition and were absorbed into Indian practice in the 20th century (S5 states the names do not occur in the classical texts; S6 follows the Western system). Cultural framing in the app should respect that: the 'shubh chinh' (auspicious marks) layer is the genuinely classical part.

## Lines (rekha)

### Life line (vitality line) — जीवन रेखा (Jeevan Rekha)
`life` · confidence **high** · tier major

Curves round the thumb-base (Venus mount). Reads vitality, energy and robustness of constitution, plus major life changes. Cheiro also claims it shows natural length of life, which modern Indian sources reject: use the 'vitality' reading only.

Aliases: पितृ रेखा (Pitru Rekha) — some Hindi books; Vivahika lists Pitri/Ayur for the life line; आयु रेखा (Ayu Rekha) — alias in some sources; in others a separate classical 'age' line, see classical_ayu_line

*Note:* The user's naming (life=pitru, head=matru, heart=ayu) is one tradition's labelling. Sources conflict, so the app should show Jeevan / Mastishk / Hridaya / Bhagya as primary labels and treat the others as per-source aliases.
