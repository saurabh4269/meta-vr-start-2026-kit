# 2026 public submissions — aggregate view only

**No individual 2026 project is named or linked here, on purpose.** This page only summarises patterns in the public Devpost gallery data gathered on 14–16 Nov 2026 (before the deadline), so you can see which idea spaces are crowded and which are open.

Method: 382 gallery entries whose Devpost page carried this competition's badge. "Built with" tags come from Devpost; flags and themes come from keyword matching on titles, taglines and descriptions, so they're approximate and overlap (one project can count in several rows). Track and division were mostly not shown publicly, so they aren't broken down. Percentages are rounded.

## General patterns
- Nearly everyone claims "hands-first" (81%), and over half claim seated, AI or MR. Saying it isn't a differentiator; showing it in the first 10 seconds of the video is.
- AI features appear in ~59% of entries. Thin chat-window wrappers run into the rules' "take it away test".
- Accessibility is *mentioned* by ~27% but is the *core purpose* of only ~4%, so the space is open for entries where accessibility is the point and not a checkbox.
- FoV-aware layout and the 60 fps bar are explicit judging items (rules §6). They weren't measured in this keyword pass, but they're cheap ways to show Technical Implementation in the video and description.
