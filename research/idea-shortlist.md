## Quick fit check per idea (from the rules)
For any pick, confirm: hands-only end-to-end · seated within 2 ft · ≤10 min satisfying loop · purposeful passthrough (the real room changes the experience) · MRUK anchoring that holds up in unseen rooms · FoV-aware UI · ≥60 fps · no logos/brands/real people. See [accessibility-and-pitfalls.md](accessibility-and-pitfalls.md) and [../setup/submission-checklist.md](../setup/submission-checklist.md).
