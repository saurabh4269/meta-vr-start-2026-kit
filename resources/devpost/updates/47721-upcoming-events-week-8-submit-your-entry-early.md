Submit your entry early; you can keep editing until the deadline.

##### 

##### ⚠️ After the deadline, everything is locked.

Once the Entry Period ends, you can’t change your submission form, build, video, or hosted URL. Judging is based on your Entry as it stands at the deadline, and changes during judging can put your eligibility at risk. Keep your Project available and accessible throughout judging (Nov 23 - Dec 9) so judges can launch it. If you want to keep building, work in a separate copy.
