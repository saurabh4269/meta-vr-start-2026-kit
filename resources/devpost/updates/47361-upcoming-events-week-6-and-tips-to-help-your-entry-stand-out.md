## [Upcoming Events: Week 6 and Tips to help your entry stand out](/updates/47361-upcoming-events-week-6-and-tips-to-help-your-entry-stand-out)

Hi Developers,

With *about three weeks left in the competition*, now’s the time to finish, test, and polish. Next week’s sessions can help you clear blockers, find bugs, and get fresh eyes on your experience before you submit.

##### 🎯 Make your entry stand out

* **Finish the core experience.** A focused, satisfying experience is a stronger showcase than a bigger one with unfinished features. Prioritize the interactions that make your project worth putting on a headset for.
* **Test with fresh eyes.** Ask someone new to try your build without coaching. Can they get started, understand the interactions, and enjoy the experience? Use their feedback to guide your final fixes.
* **Show what makes it yours.** Give your assets a consistent style, and open your submission video with real gameplay that makes your hands-first design clear in the first 30 seconds.
