## [Give yourself a schedule | Save the date: LIVE Build Session October 13](/updates/46826-give-yourself-a-schedule-save-the-date-live-build-session-october-13)

##### **While you wait, here’s how to use the next two weeks.**

⛈️ **Brainstorm**—this week  
💡 **Hone your idea, define your track and division**—by Friday, October 9  
🎨 **Sketch your interaction model or updates**—week of October 12

**Pick your track.** Entertainment, Gaming, or Productivity.   
Full descriptions are on [the competition site](https://start-developer-competition-26.devpost.com/).

**Pick your division.** *New Experience* means starting from a blank slate on or after   
September 24, 2026.  *Adapted/Significantly Updated Experience* means a pre-existing project with a meaningful new feature, mode, or platform integration shipped during the competition window.  If you're adapting, start a changelog today. You'll be asked to summarize exactly what you added, and reconstructing that from memory in November is harder than it sounds.

**Design for hands.** Hand tracking is what makes these experiences feel intuitive—no controller to pick up, no barrier between the person and the thing they're doing. Assume empty hands in every interaction, and favor gestures people already understand: grab, point, tap, push.

**Optimize for seated and stationary.** Keep everything within comfortable arm's reach, put the action in front of the player rather than behind them, and avoid anything that requires standing, stepping, or large sweeping motions. Design for a seated field of view and your experience works everywhere.

**Make the first 10 minutes satisfying.** Judges and players should be able to put on the headset, understand what to do without a tutorial wall, and reach a genuinely enjoyable moment all inside 10 minutes. Get them into the experience fast and make sure the core loop lands early rather than after a long ramp-up.
