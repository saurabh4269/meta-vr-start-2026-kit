## [Join Us for the 1st Competition Kickoff & Team Formation](/updates/46693-join-us-for-the-1st-competition-kickoff-team-formation)

![](https://devpost-public.s3.amazonaws.com/Meta%20Banner%20%28Merged%20-%2002%29.png)

Today's the day. Join us for **Assemble your Party**, the official kickoff of the Meta VR Start Developer Competition. It's also a great chance to be introduced to teammates before building gets underway.

We're hosting **two sessions,** one for each region. Both cover the same content, so join whichever time works best for your schedule:

 🌍 **EMEA:** Tuesday, September 29 at **3pm CET**

 🌎 **Americas:** Tuesday, September 29 at **10am (PDT)**

**👉 Register here:**

* [Click here to Register for the EMEA Session](https://communityforums.atmeta.com/event/start_events/assemble-your-party-competition-kickoff--team-formation-emea/1384391)
* [Click here to Register for the North America Session](https://communityforums.atmeta.com/event/start_events/assemble-your-party-competition-kickoff--team-formation-noram/1384389)

##### 

##### What we'll cover

* **The competition at a glance:** the three tracks (Entertainment, Gaming, and Productivity), the two divisions, and what judges are looking for
* **What "hands-first" really means:** the one requirement that makes or breaks an entry
* **Key dates and how to submit:** so nothing catches you off guard in November
* **Team formation:** meet other builders to find people whose skills complement yours
* **Live Q&A:** bring your questions

##### **Come ready to team up 🤝**

Looking for teammates? Before you join, take a couple minutes to prepare:

* Flag that you're looking for a team on the [Developers Forum](https://communityforums.atmeta.com/category/horizon-developer-forum) and [Participants tab](https://start-developer-competition-26.devpost.com/participants)
* Have a one-line intro ready: your skills (ie: Unity, Unreal, WebXR, design, audio, video), and the track you're leaning toward

Once you've found your party, select **Start Project** and use **Manage Team** to invite them. Make sure everyone accepts their invite.

##### 

##### Not a Meta VR Start member yet?

You, or the submitting member of your team, must be an approved member by the time you submit. Approval isn't instant, so [apply today](https://developers.meta.com/vr/discover/programs/start/).

See every upcoming workshop and office hour on the [events calendar](https://start-developer-competition-26.devpost.com/details/webinars-office-hours), and ask questions any time on the [Meta Developer Forum](https://communityforums.atmeta.com/category/horizon-developer-forum).

See you there!
