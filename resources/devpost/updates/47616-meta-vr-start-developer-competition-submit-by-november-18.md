## [Meta VR Start Developer Competition | Submit by November 18](/updates/47616-meta-vr-start-developer-competition-submit-by-november-18)

Hi Developers,

Submissions for the **Meta VR Start Developer Competition close November 18 at 12:00 p.m. (PST)**. Submit your entry early; you can keep editing until the deadline.

Before you submit, check these essentials:

* **Hands-first.** Test the full experience without controllers, including onboarding and menus.
* **Judge access.** Upload your APK to a new release channel named “Competition,” or provide a hosted URL for IWSDK/WebXR. Have someone outside your team test access.
* **Demo video.** Share a public YouTube or Vimeo video under three minutes showing your experience on Quest or an equivalent simulator.
* **Devpost entry.** Complete every required field, select your track and division, and explain what’s new if you’re adapting an existing app. The person submitting must be an approved Start member. Confirm your project is marked Submitted, not Draft.

Your entry cannot change after the deadline. Keep it free and accessible to judges until the winner announcement.

##### Need help finishing your entry?

* [Submission and development guides](https://start-developer-competition-26.devpost.com/resources): Release-channel setup, WebXR hosting, SDKs, and samples.
* [Hands tutorial recording](https://communityforums.atmeta.com/community-resources-25/video-how-to-onboard-players-for-vr-hand-controls-180785): Help new players learn hand controls. Start sign-in required.
* [Meta VR CLI performance tooling](https://developers.meta.com/horizon/essentials/metavr-performance): Check performance before your final upload.
* [Webinars, recordings, and office hours](https://start-developer-competition-26.devpost.com/details/webinars-office-hours): Get mentor guidance on your remaining blockers.

We look forward to seeing what you build!
