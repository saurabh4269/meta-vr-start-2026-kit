## [Sign up for an Upcoming Virtual Session!](/updates/46687-sign-up-for-an-upcoming-virtual-session)

**The Meta VR Start Developer Competition has begun.**

Jump in and get started with one of these upcoming live sessions!

This week, the Competition will kick off its weekly Virtual Sessions by hosting the first two **LIVE**. Each session is offered twice so you can join from either region.

![](https://devpost-public.s3.amazonaws.com/ribbon%20reminder.jpeg)As a reminder, to attend these events, you will need to be a registered Meta VR Start member *and* logged into the Developer Forum with the account associated to your Start membership.

* If you need to create for your **Meta VR STart Membership**, please [click here to apply](https://developers.meta.com/vr/discover/programs/start/)
* If you need to register for the **Meta Developer Forum**, click [here](https://communityforums.atmeta.com/category/horizon-developer-forum).

##### EVENT 1: Assemble your Party: Competition Kickoff & Team Formation

**Date: Tuesday, Sep 29, 2026**

* NORAM — 10:00 AM PT · [Click here to Register for the North America Session](https://communityforums.atmeta.com/event/start_events/assemble-your-party-competition-kickoff--team-formation-noram/1384389)
* EMEA — 3:00 PM CET · [Click here to Register for the EMEA Session](https://communityforums.atmeta.com/event/start_events/assemble-your-party-competition-kickoff--team-formation-emea/1384391)

***About the Event:***   
![](https://devpost-public.s3.amazonaws.com/Emojis/alarm-clock_23f0.png)  To kick things off, Meta VR Start Mentor, Tevfik will walk through the full competition structure: the two divisions (a new app, or a significant update to a title you've already shipped), what you hand over by the November 18 deadline, and where to find mentor support along the way. Then you go straight into live matchmaking.

![](https://devpost-public.s3.amazonaws.com/Emojis/busts-in-silhouette_1f465.png)  Come with a team or without one. You'll leave with a team or a direct route to one, an eight-week roadmap with weekly checkpoints, and a written setup guide covering roles, tools, and code sharing.

##### **EVENT 2:** **Fast Essentials: Ideation — Find Your Competition Concept**

**Date: Thursday, Oct 1, 2026**

* NORAM — 10:00 AM PT · [Click here to Register for Event 2 - "Fast Essentials" North America Session](https://zoom.us/meeting/register/l_v5SJEtTayFY4ZI4ndDVg#/registration)
* EMEA — 3:00 PM CET · [Click here to Register for Event 2 - "Fast Essentials" EMEA Session](https://zoom.us/meeting/register/ZKf0vhzwR0-TunztQpj6Cw#/registration)

**About the Event:**   
Half workshop, half working session with the Meta VR Start mentor team. Eight weeks is too long to spend building the wrong thing.

👷  You'll generate a few concepts instead of committing to the first one, work out which fits your track, and define the one core interaction your entry is built around. Updating an app you've already shipped? Same process, applied to the feature you're adding. Then you pitch in breakouts and get real feedback, so nobody leaves without a direction.

🧰  You'll leave with a written concept that fits your track and your timeline, one core interaction defined, and at least one honest reaction to your idea before you start building.
