#### 🤝 Looking for teammates?

* **Join live:** attend sessions to meet fellow builders, swap ideas, and get introduced to potential teammates in real time. These will be  the only sessions where live team introductions happen, so pick the one that fits your region and join live.
* **Can’t make it live?** Head to the [Participants tab](https://start-developer-competition-26.devpost.com/participants) anytime. Flag that you’re looking for teammates, filter by skills, and message people directly through Devpost.

**Missed a session?** No problem. Recorded sessions will be added to the [Webinars tab](https://start-developer-competition-26.devpost.com/details/webinars-office-hours) within one week, so you can catch up whenever it suits you.

**Looking for help?** Join an office hour or live educational session from the [events calendar](https://start-developer-competition-26.devpost.com/details/webinars-office-hours), or ask a question anytime on the [Meta Developer Forum](https://communityforums.atmeta.com/category/horizon-developer-forum).
