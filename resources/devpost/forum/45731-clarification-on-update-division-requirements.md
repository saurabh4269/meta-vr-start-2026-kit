### Clarification on Update Division Requirements

Hello,

I'm planning to enter the Adapted / Significantly Updated Experience division with an existing Meta Quest app.

The FAQ states that publishing to the Meta Quest Store is not required and recommends using a private Alpha channel for updated apps.

However, the division requirements state that a meaningful update must be "shipped during the competition window."

Could you please clarify the following?

Does submitting the updated build through a private release channel satisfy the "shipped" requirement, or must the update be publicly released on the Meta Quest Store?

Can we submit our Devpost entry first and update the build afterward, as long as the updated build is available to judges before the submission deadline?

Thank you for your help!

  If you are using an existing project you must have made significant updates to it during the submission period.

  Those instructions are based on you having issues with a URL: having trouble creating a shareable URL from the Developer Center for my submission, or my accessible build is past the 90-day limit. If this is your situation, then yes, draft your updated app as a new project with a different name in the Developer Center and use a private Alpha Channel to upload your latest competition build.

  Yes, you can update your game/app up until the competition closes at 12:00PM PST on November 18, 2026. Please ensure your app remains available and you do not make any updates during the judging period between November 18 at 12:00PM PST and when winners are announced on or around December 11. See Official Rules.
