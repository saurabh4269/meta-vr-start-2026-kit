### Rule 19 question — recognizable real engine design with logos/names removed

Hello — a Rule 19 question. Item 19 bars 'recognizable branded products' and corporate logos/brand names. Our educational project teaches how a real, documented production engine works, using the manufacturer's published documentation. We would show no logos, brand names, or advertising, but the engine's design itself could be recognizable to an enthusiast. Does item 19 prohibit a recognizable real product design even with all logos and names removed? If not, is removing badges/names sufficient, or would we need a license from the manufacturer — and would written proof of permission, if we obtained it, satisfy the rule (noting the rules allow the Sponsor to request written proof of license)? We are asking before modeling, not after. Thank you.
