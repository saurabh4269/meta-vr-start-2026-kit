### Clarification: Three.js WebXR submission and educational engine models

Could the organizers please clarify two points in the 2026 official rules?

1. The FAQ permits another engine and a URL for web experiences, while Section 4 names IWSDK (WebXR) for hosted submissions. May an application built directly with Three.js and WebXR, without IWSDK, submit a hosted HTTPS URL, provided it works hands-first on Quest 3/3S? Or is using IWSDK mandatory for this route?

2. Section 4(19) excludes recognizable branded products. Does that exclude an accurately modeled real engine used for mechanical education even if it has no logos, manufacturer names or promotion? Is a generic original V8 teaching model the appropriate route? Can factual source citations or compatible-parts information name manufacturers, or must those references also be omitted from the entry?

Thank you. A written clarification would help us select the correct implementation and content before asset production.
