### Rule 4(19): entrant-owned garment concepts in a wardrobe-planning prototype

Hello Meta VR Start Developer Competition team,

We are preparing a hands-first IWSDK wardrobe-planning prototype. It lets someone compare two original garment concepts, save and revise a personal edit, and optionally take a short quiet interval. It has no checkout or purchase links. The current concept images include the entrant's own small clothing mark and are attributed to the entrant's public concept posts.

Rule 4(19) restricts commercial or corporate advertising, brand names/logos and recognizable branded products. Does that restriction also prohibit an entrant's own original garment designs and clothing mark when they are the subject of the app rather than a sponsor advertisement?

If an entrant-owned exception is allowed, what ownership or permission evidence should accompany the entry, and are links to the original public concept posts permitted? If no exception applies, would separately created, properly licensed fictional garment studies without brand marks be an acceptable app-content route, subject to the other rules?

We are asking before finalizing the competition build and video. We will not imply an association with Meta or treat a general IP-ownership permission as clearance of the advertising rule.

Thank you.
