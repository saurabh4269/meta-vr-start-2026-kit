##### Development Setup

* [Set up your headset for development](https://developers.meta.com/horizon/documentation/unity/unity-env-device-setup)

##### Submission Setup

* [Set up Release Channels](https://developers.meta.com/horizon/resources/publish-release-channels) (required for Unity, Unreal, Godot, Android/Native and other competition submission)
* Deploying IWSDK (WebXR) [through GitHub Pages](https://developers.meta.com/horizon/documentation/web/iwsdk-guide-build-deploy#deploying-to-github-pages) or [Vercel](https://github.com/meta-quest/iwsdk-v0-template) (required for IWSDK (WebXR) competition submission)
