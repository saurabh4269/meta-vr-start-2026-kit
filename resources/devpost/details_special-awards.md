#### **Special Awards**

Open to both new and adapted / significantly updated experiences across all tracks. Experiment with the latest Meta VR features and toolkits, and get rewarded for innovation, design, and technical excellence:

* **Best Social & Multiplayer Experience**—Built for shared presence: co-watching, cooperative play, social spaces, asymmetric multiplayer.
* **Best Agentic Interaction**—AI as a core part of the experience: spatial agents, voice-first interaction, procedural content, context-aware assistance.
* **Best Reason to Come Back**—Thoughtful progression and reward design: something to work toward, visible growth between sessions, a loop that pulls the user back tomorrow. Built for longevity, not a one-time demo.
* **Best First Five Minutes**—The craft of the opening: how fast it explains itself, teaches controller-free interaction without a wall of text, and delivers a payoff before the headset comes off.
* **Best Accessibility Forward Experience**—Pushing accessible XR design: voice alternatives for hand interactions, eyes-only navigation for limited mobility, high-contrast modes, inclusive by default.
* **Boldest Original Concept**—Rewards the idea that couldn't exist anywhere else. A concept whose core loop depends on presence, spatial depth, real-world scale, or eyes and hands. Judged on originality and on whether the experience would fall apart on any other platform.
* **Judge’s Choice**—Awarded at the judges’ discretion for standout entries that don’t fit neatly into a single track or category.
