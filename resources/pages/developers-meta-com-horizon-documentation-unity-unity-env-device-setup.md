Develop

Updated: Oct 8, 2026

Verify that your development computer and other hardware meet the requirements for the testing method you plan to use. You configure your Meta VR device in [Software Setup](https://developers.meta.com/vr/documentation/unity/unity-software-setup/).

Make sure you have the right hardware before you start building. You will need a development computer and, depending on your testing method, a Meta VR device and USB-C cable.

| Hardware | Meta XR Simulator | Meta Horizon Link | Build and Run | 
|---|---|---|---|
| **Development computer** | Windows or Apple Silicon macOS | Windows only | Windows or macOS | 
| **Meta VR device** | *Not needed* | Required | Required | 
| **USB-C cable** | *Not needed* | [See Link requirements](https://www.meta.com/help/quest/articles/headsets-and-accessories/oculus-link/requirements-quest-link/) or use Wi-Fi | Data-capable USB-C cable required | 

Windows

macOS

On Windows, you can also use [Meta Horizon Link](https://developers.meta.com/vr/documentation/unity/unity-link/) to stream your app directly to a Meta VR device without building an APK. This is useful for faster iteration with real hand tracking. See [Link requirements](https://www.meta.com/help/quest/articles/headsets-and-accessories/oculus-link/requirements-quest-link/) for cable guidance and other details.

After you confirm the requirements for your selected testing method, your hardware is ready. Continue to [Software Setup](https://developers.meta.com/vr/documentation/unity/unity-software-setup/).
