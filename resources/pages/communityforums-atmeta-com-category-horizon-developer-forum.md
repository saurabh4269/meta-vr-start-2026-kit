🎮 **The Meta VR Start Developer Competition Is Back!**  

🖐️ The future of VR is in your hands—literally. The **Meta VR Start Developer Competition** is an eight-week challenge inviting Start program members to build **hands-first experiences** for Meta VR devices, including Meta VR Glasses and Meta Quest and compete for a share of **$1M in cash prizes**.

- 🕹️ **Build in one of three tracks:** Gaming, Entertainment, or Productivity
- ✨ **Enter in either division:** New Experience or Adapted/Significantly Updated Experience
- 🏅 **Go further for bonus awards** like Best Social & Multiplayer Experience, Best Agentic Interaction, and Best Accessibility Forward Interaction
- 📅 **Key dates:** Submissions are open **September 24–November 18, 2026**; winners are announced on or around **December 11, 2026**

🚀 Whether you’re a first-time Start developer with a breakthrough idea or a seasoned pro ready to push the latest platform capabilities, **this is your moment to help define the future of VR**.

Check out the [<u>full announcement post</u>](https://communityforums.atmeta.com/discussions/start-events-and-competitions/meta-vr-start-developer-competition-2026/1384075) and [<u>Devpost page</u>](https://start-developer-competition-26.devpost.com/?preview_token=ZNg4M74wrmItOObG2n6y0%2FkmtPlbMBO6PBsWT%2FbfFRQ%3D) for divisions, tracks, judging criteria, and [<u>official rules</u>](https://start-developer-competition-26.devpost.com/rules).
