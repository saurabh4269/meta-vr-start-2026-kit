Join the battle, team up with friends, and conquer the world! By developing multiplayer games, you can create a social experience that brings people together and ignites a sense of community.

- **Establish a shared reality:** Create an overlapping experience that fosters a common understanding among users.
- **Make co-location necessary:** Design experiences that are fundamentally changed or enhanced by multiple users being physically present together.
- **Engage users with meaningful goals:** Incorporate elements of competition, cooperation, learning, and social sharing to create interactive and immersive experiences that bring users together.
