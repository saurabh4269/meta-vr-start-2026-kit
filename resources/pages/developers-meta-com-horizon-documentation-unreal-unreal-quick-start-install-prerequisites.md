To develop Meta Horizon OS apps with Unreal Engine, you must meet the following prerequisites before installing and configuring your environment:

- [Hardware and software that meets or exceeds minimum requirements](https://developers.meta.com#hardware-requirements)
- [A verified Meta developer account](https://developers.meta.com#create-dev-account)
- [Epic Games and GitHub accounts that are linked](https://developers.meta.com#link-epic-github)
- [Membership in a development team](https://developers.meta.com#join-org)

After completing these prerequisites, you can proceed to installing Unreal Engine and configuring the rest of your development environment.

To develop for Meta Horizon OS, you need a development PC and a Meta Quest device or Meta VR Glasses. This section discusses the hardware and software requirements so you can begin development.

You can develop Meta Horizon OS apps on the following operating systems:

- Windows
- macOS
- Linux

For PC system requirements, see [Hardware and Software Specifications](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/en-us/unreal-engine/hardware-and-software-specifications-for-unreal-engine) in the Unreal Engine documentation.

Plan for at least 50 GB of free disk space for an Unreal Engine installation through the Epic Games Launcher. Source builds from the Meta fork require additional space.

To test on a Meta Quest device, you need the following VR hardware:

- Meta Quest series headset
- Meta Quest Touch controllers
- USB-C cable

Meta VR Glasses ship without controllers. For Meta VR Glasses configuration and simulator testing, see [Support Meta VR Glasses](https://developers.meta.com/vr/documentation/unreal/unreal-support-meta-vr-glasses/).

1. Create a Meta developer account by following [Creating a Meta developer account](https://developers.meta.com/vr/sign-up/).
2. While logged in, verify your account at [Verifying your Meta account](https://developers.meta.com/vr/manage/verify/). Provide one of the following:
  - A valid mobile phone number for SMS two-factor authentication. Your country and carrier must support SMS push notifications. For a list of supported countries and carriers, see [Set up texts](https://www.facebook.com/help/130694300342171).
  - A credit or debit card for verification purposes.
 **Note**: PayPal is not supported for verifying an account.
3. Confirm that you have successfully verified your account by using the[verification page](https://developers.meta.com/vr/manage/verify/).

You also need an Epic Games account and a GitHub account. Link these accounts after you set them up.

1. Create and verify an [Epic Games account](https://accounts.unrealengine.com/).
2. Sign up for a GitHub account at [GitHub](https://github.com/).
3. Link your Epic Games and GitHub accounts at [Accessing Unreal Source Code on GitHub](https://www.unrealengine.com/en-US/ue-on-github).

Your Meta developer account must be part of a Meta-registered team to develop Meta Horizon OS apps. You can join an existing team or create a new one for your work.

If the team already exists, contact a team admin and request that your developer account be added. Your account joins the team automatically after approval.

You can also create a new team if one does not exist. You can create a new team in the [Developer Dashboard](https://developers.meta.com/vr/manage/).

1. In the [Developer Dashboard](https://developers.meta.com/vr/manage/), under **Org Manager**, click **Select Team** and then click **Create**.
2. Choose a name for your team. This should be the legal name of your team, and not a nickname. Meta uses this as the developer display name for any app that your team publishes to the Meta Horizon Store.**Note**: Once you create your team’s name, you *cannot* change it from the Developer Dashboard.
3. Double-check your team name to ensure you are satisfied with the name you entered.
4. Select the **I agree** box, and then click **Submit**.
5. Review the **Developer Non-Disclosure Agreement** and then click **I Agree** to accept it.
6. Click **Submit** to finish.
